Delete Your Faeros Account

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

You can permanently delete your Faeros account and associated personal data at any time.

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Faeros and sign in.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner of the home screen.
  3. Choose Account Settings.
  4. Tap Delete Account, type DELETE, and confirm.

If you have bounties in progress, finish or cancel them first. The app will tell you if anything is blocking deletion.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, email us from the address registered to your account with the subject "Delete my Faeros account". We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, username, bio, profile picture)
  • Your sign-in credentials and linked Google sign-in
  • Your messages, notifications, friends and group memberships
  • Videos and media you uploaded
  • Your saved location and push-notification token
  • Comments you wrote in ratings
  • Your Stripe customer / payout-account link, where one exists

What may be kept

  • Transaction and payment records, retained for up to 7 years for legal and tax purposes. These are disconnected from your profile and no longer show your name.
  • Star ratings you gave or received (without comments), so other users' reputation scores remain accurate. They are no longer linked to a name or profile.
  • Data we are legally required to keep, or that is needed to prevent fraud or abuse.

Location history

Your saved location is removed immediately when your account is deleted.
